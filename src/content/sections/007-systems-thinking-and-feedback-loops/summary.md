<strong>Core idea.</strong> Outcomes often emerge from interactions among stocks, flows, feedback, delays, constraints and incentives. A system map is a testable explanation of those interactions, not proof of a single inevitable outcome.

<strong>Essential terms.</strong> System; boundary; element; interaction; emergent behavior; complex; stock; flow; buffer; reinforcing loop; balancing loop; causal link; delay; oscillation; nonlinearity; threshold; hysteresis; path dependence; network; bottleneck; redundancy; common-pool resource; externality; security dilemma; rebound; Goodhart's law; leverage point; sensitivity analysis; scenario.

<strong>Explain it aloud.</strong> Choose either a crowded railway, a shop with empty shelves, or two rival states adding defensive weapons. Describe the important stocks and flows, at least one reinforcing or balancing loop, a delay or bottleneck, and an intervention whose side effects you would want to test.
