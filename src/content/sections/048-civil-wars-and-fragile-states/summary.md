<strong>Civil war.</strong> Sustained organized armed conflict within a political community.

<strong>State capacity.</strong> The ability of institutions to carry out public decisions.

<strong>Legitimacy.</strong> The accepted or justified right to govern.

<strong>Fragility.</strong> Exposure to risks combined with insufficient resilience.

<strong>Grievance.</strong> Actual or perceived injustice or exclusion.

<strong>Horizontal inequality.</strong> Disparities between socially meaningful groups.

<strong>War economy.</strong> Economic relationships reshaped by ongoing conflict.

<strong>Internationalization.</strong> External involvement in a conflict with internal origins.

<strong>Displacement.</strong> Forced departure from home, internally or across borders.

<strong>Credible commitment.</strong> A promise supported by dependable institutions or guarantees.

<strong>Transitional justice.</strong> Addressing violations through truth, justice, reparations and reform.

<strong>Positive peace.</strong> Conditions supporting peaceful and just social relations.
