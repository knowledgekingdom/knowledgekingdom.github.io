<strong>Diplomacy.</strong> Authorised representation and negotiation of international relationships.

<strong>Sovereignty.</strong> Legal authority and status; distinguish from effective control.

<strong>Bargaining range.</strong> The set of acceptable outcomes relative to alternatives.

<strong>Two-level games.</strong> International agreement and domestic acceptance interact.

<strong>Alliance.</strong> A security cooperation commitment, not universal policy agreement.

<strong>Balance of power.</strong> Distribution of capabilities or policies intended to prevent dominance.

<strong>Security dilemma.</strong> Reciprocal insecurity generated by uncertainty and protective actions.

<strong>Collective security.</strong> An institutional response to threats to peace; distinct from collective defence.
