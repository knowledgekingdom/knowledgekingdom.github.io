<strong>Popular sovereignty</strong> means that the ultimate source of legitimate political authority is located in the people rather than solely in a hereditary ruler. <strong>Constitutional government</strong> limits and organizes public power by law. <strong>Natural rights</strong> are claims presented as belonging to persons prior to a ruler's grant. <strong>Republic</strong> describes a political order without hereditary monarchy, but does not by itself guarantee inclusive democracy. <strong>Emancipation</strong> means release from slavery or another legally enforced subjection. <strong>Political citizenship</strong> concerns a person's recognized membership, legal protections, and capacity to participate in public decisions; its boundaries vary historically.
