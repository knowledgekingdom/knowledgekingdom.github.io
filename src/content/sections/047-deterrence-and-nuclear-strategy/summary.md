<strong>Deterrence.</strong> Influencing decisions to prevent an action.

<strong>Mutual vulnerability.</strong> Shared exposure to catastrophic nuclear harm.

<strong>Strategic stability.</strong> Conditions reducing the risk of catastrophic confrontation.

<strong>Security dilemma.</strong> Reciprocal fear can intensify competition.

<strong>Nuclear taboo.</strong> Normative inhibition against nuclear use.

<strong>Proliferation.</strong> Spread or expansion of nuclear weapons.

<strong>NPT.</strong> Non-proliferation, peaceful uses and disarmament.

<strong>Safeguards.</strong> Verification of specified commitments.

<strong>Arms control.</strong> Agreed restrictions and measures to reduce risk.

<strong>Humanitarian consequences.</strong> Immediate and long-term harm extending beyond national borders.

<strong>Disarmament.</strong> Reduction and elimination of weapons and capabilities.
