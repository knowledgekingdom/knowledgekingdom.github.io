<strong>Political purpose.</strong> The desired political outcome; military activity is one means rather than an end in itself.

<strong>Military capability.</strong> The conditional ability to perform a defined task.

<strong>Civilian control.</strong> Accountability of the armed forces to lawful political institutions.

<strong>Logistics and sustainment.</strong> The provision and movement of resources, and maintenance of institutional functioning over time.

<strong>Readiness.</strong> A task-dependent assessment of whether an institution can perform at the required standard and time.

<strong>Interoperability.</strong> The capacity of distinct institutions to work effectively together.

<strong>Opportunity cost.</strong> The value of the best alternative foregone when resources are allocated to a particular purpose.
