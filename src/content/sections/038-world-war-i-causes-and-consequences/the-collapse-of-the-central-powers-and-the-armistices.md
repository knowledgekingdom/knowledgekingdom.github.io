Bulgaria's exit was followed by other major collapses. The Ottoman Empire signed the Armistice of Mudros on 30 October 1918. Austria-Hungary fragmented as national councils and movements proclaimed new political arrangements, and its armistice with Italy was signed in early November. Germany faced worsening military conditions, social unrest and a revolution that ended the Hohenzollern monarchy. An armistice between Germany and the Allies took effect on 11 November 1918. It ended fighting on the principal Western Front, but it did not instantly end violence throughout Europe or the former Ottoman lands.

An armistice is not a peace treaty. It suspends fighting under military conditions; it does not necessarily resolve sovereignty, reparations, prisoners or the legal status of disputed regions. The political collapse of multinational empires was also not a neat replacement of old borders by self-evident national ones. Borderlands included overlapping populations, armed movements and outside interventions.
