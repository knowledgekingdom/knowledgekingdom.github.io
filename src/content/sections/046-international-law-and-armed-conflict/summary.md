<strong>Legal obligation.</strong> A binding rule, distinct from jurisdiction and enforcement.

<strong>Jus ad bellum.</strong> Law governing the resort to force.

<strong>Jus in bello.</strong> Law governing conduct during armed conflict, independently of political cause.

<strong>Conflict classification.</strong> International or non-international armed conflict, based on the facts.

<strong>Civilian protection.</strong> Distinction, proportionality and precautions are separate duties.

<strong>Occupation.</strong> Effective authority without transfer of sovereignty.

<strong>State responsibility.</strong> Attribution, breach and consequences under international law.

<strong>Individual criminal responsibility.</strong> Personal liability for the elements of a recognized offense.

<strong>Jurisdiction.</strong> The legal authority of a particular court to decide a case.

<strong>Complementarity.</strong> The ICC supplements rather than generally replaces national proceedings.

<strong>Procedural literacy.</strong> Distinguish a warrant, provisional order, advisory opinion and final judgment.
