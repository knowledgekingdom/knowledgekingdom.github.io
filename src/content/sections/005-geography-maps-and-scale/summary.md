<strong>Core idea.</strong> Geography studies physical and human patterns across space and time. Location influences possibilities and costs, but institutions, technology and human choices change what locations mean.

<strong>Six questions.</strong> Where is it? What scale and date are we using? Which physical and human processes matter? How is it connected to other places? What evidence supports the claim? Which alternative explanations remain?

<strong>Essential terms.</strong> Physical geography; human geography; place; scale; projection; Mercator; equal-area; latitude; longitude; datum; great circle; watershed; drainage divide; plate tectonics; rain shadow; Hadley cell; monsoon; ocean current; upwelling; biome; accessibility; agglomeration; chokepoint; GIS; choropleth; ecological fallacy; MAUP; geographical determinism.
