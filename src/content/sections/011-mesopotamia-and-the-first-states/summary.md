<strong>Core argument.</strong> Some of the earliest cities and states arose within the long social and ecological history of southern Mesopotamia. Their development depended on multiple interacting processes: irrigation and farming, settlement concentration, trade, religious institutions, administration, political rivalry, and changing systems of authority. Writing expanded their capacity to manage information, but was neither the sole cause of cities nor proof of universal literacy.
